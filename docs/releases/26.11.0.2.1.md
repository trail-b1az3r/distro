## What's new in Rain Field 26.11.0.2.1

**Nexora is now Frontline Linux.** A new name and a new mark: a hand-drawn snow-capped peak in ink and watercolour
on a square tile, on the boot menu, the boot splash, the login screen, the icons and Fastfetch (as Braille line art).
The colours follow it: graphite, slate blue and sage. The command is `frontline` (`nexora` still works), the
packages are `frontline-*`, and the repository is `[frontline]`.

**A new login screen: [SilentSDDM](https://github.com/uiriansan/SilentSDDM)** (GPL-3.0) as it ships, its own look line
for line: a lock screen with the clock, then the login over the picture blurred, with your picture, session, keyboard
layout and power menus. The picture is Carol M. Highsmith's photograph of the Tetons (public domain). The earlier login
screen stays installed as "Frontline Linux Classic". Installing HyDE no longer takes the login screen over (its
setting was read after ours); this update puts it back if it did.

**Updating from Nexora 26.11.0.2.0** is `sudo pacman -Syu` as always: pacman asks once to replace the `nexora-*`
packages with `frontline-*` (answer yes), then moves everything over by itself: pacman.conf's repositories,
`/etc/nexora`, your settings folders (`~/.config/nexora-write` and the like), enabled timers, the login screen and
the boot splash. The old paths stay as links, and nothing is deleted.

**HyperNeo v2, with Summit.** A new Halcyon theme with a shell of its own: Summit, clean and quiet, everything frosted
glass. One slim top bar of three floating groups (the launcher and workspaces; the clock and the weather; the tray,
network, sound, battery and power; the battery opens the power mode and the super battery saver), a dock, Rofi menus for apps (tap Super), windows, the clipboard, power and every
keybind (Super+/), its own theme picker in pages (Super+Shift+T), and springy motion throughout. The weather is where
you are, found from the Wi-Fi networks around you (BeaconDB, else your IP; `halcyon weather location ip` keeps the
networks to yourself), with a forecast window. Over the Tetons at noon.

**Liquid Glass, with MacShell.** A new Halcyon theme that swaps the whole shell for
[MacShell](https://github.com/0-ss/MacShell) (MIT, bundled): a macOS Tahoe style menu bar, Dock, Control Center and
Spotlight in real-time Liquid Glass that refracts the wallpaper, with MacLock as the lock screen. Any other theme
brings Halcyon's shell back. Super+Shift+T opens MacShell's own theme picker, in pages. Tapping Super opens Spotlight
(it used to open fuzzel on every Super press, which also swallowed Super+W and the other Super keys).

**Settings search.** Settings has a search at the top (Ctrl+F): it finds any setting by its name or what it does.

**Theme picker.** The themes come in pages, and the selection no longer jumps back to the current theme while you
move it.

**The wall between monitors, for tiled windows** (hyprland-plus 0.57.2.p3). A tiled window dragged onto another
monitor is now held at the edge like a floating one, the other monitor's edge bulges where it would come in, and when
the wall gives the window glides on instead of jumping. Window resistance holds tiled drags at the screen's edges.

**Choose your desktop shell.** Settings > Halcyon > Desktop shell (or `halcyon shell`, or Ctrl+Super+Shift+G from any
of them) runs the shell you pick whatever the theme: Halcyon, **Halcyon Glass**, Summit, MacShell or **Noctalia
Shell**. Halcyon Glass is Halcyon's own shell with see-through panels over liquid glass: hyprland-plus 0.57.2.p4's new
`liquid` blur, after [MyGlass](https://github.com/Sidharth7082/myglass) (BSD-3-Clause), bends the wallpaper at each
panel's rim, dims what is bright behind it and lights its top edge. [Noctalia](https://github.com/noctalia-dev/noctalia)
v5 (MIT) runs inside Halcyon with Halcyon's keys and themes, and the installer offers every one of them as the Halcyon
shell. `distro-install-w-halcyon_desktop_shell-theme SHELL` installs a shell's packages and switches to it.

**Search inside your documents.** `frontline docs search WORDS` finds them by what they say, ranked, with the
passage that matched: Markdown and text, HTML, PDF, Word, OpenDocument, EPUB, PowerPoint and Excel files in Documents,
Desktop, Downloads and Notes (`docs add FOLDER` for more). `docs pick` opens one from a menu, and `docs ask QUESTION`
answers from your own documents with the local model, citing the passages it used. The index is a file on this
computer, kept up to date every half hour; nothing is sent anywhere (`docs off` stops it).

**Frontline Write** lays Document view out on real A4 pages: page breaks start a new page, text that does not fit
flows onto the next, headers and footers repeat with page numbers, and PDFs print those pages with their text kept as
text. Write's own text is drawn as vector outlines, sharp at any size.

**Neumorph.** A light theme of soft clay: the dock, the bar, the clock and the cards are pressed out of one pale page
in its own colour, lit from the top left. It comes with its own icon pack and the rounded Open Runde font.

**Islands bar.** A fourth bar style in Settings > Bar: no strip across the screen, only floating pills (the window
title, the workspaces, the clock, the tray), each with a soft shadow.

**Fonts.** Font Manager is installed, and `halcyon fonts install FILE...` installs fonts, or a zip of a whole family,
for your user. Halcyon ships **RightFont**, "the most confusing font possible" from Right of the Bell (SIL OFL):
every letter only just looks like itself.

**frontline-msg 1.1.** Your iPhone's messages over Bluetooth or the cable, now with a remote desktop viewer for this PC
(over the new `frontline-rdp-express` server) and Tailscale, so both work away from home.

**Wi-Fi (nmx 1.1).** Captive-portal networks open their sign-in page instead of asking for a password, an optional
DNS-blocker bypass (off by default), and enterprise networks find their certificate on their own.

**Faster shut down, log out and wake-up.** Apps opened from the shell no longer hold the session open for systemd's
two-minute timeout.

**The power button opens a power menu** in every desktop: lock, log out, sleep, hibernate, restart, shut down and
firmware setup, instead of switching the computer off at once. Halcyon uses its own session menu, My Linux for Work
its ML4W power menu, and the other desktops (ML4W, HyDE, SekaiPop, MacShell, Frameshell, PaperRice) get
`frontline-power-menu`. At the login screen or on a console the button still powers off.

**Spire Shell** is now its Zsharpend edition, packaged (`spire-shell`), so choosing it in the installer works offline
too; it runs your fish config as it is, and bash syntax alongside. Systems installed earlier had a build of spire's
main branch in /usr/local/bin, still their login shell: `frontline update` replaces it with the Zsharpend edition
(the old one is kept in /usr/local/lib/spire-main).

**Jarvis, an offline voice assistant.** `halcyon jarvis on`, then say "wake up Jarvis, open Firefox" (or click,
type, scroll, screenshot). It runs on this computer with Vosk: no AI model, no account, no network
([jarvis-linux](https://github.com/DwizSharma/jarvis-linux), MIT).

**OLED care.** `halcyon oled on` dims one of every four pixels with [hyproled](https://github.com/mklan/hyproled)
(BSD-3-Clause, bundled) and moves them every 15 minutes, so the bar never burns in.

**Frontline Write** underlines misspelled words (right-click for suggestions) and fixes common typos as you type.
**Frontline Code** signs git in with your GitHub CLI login and commits with your GitHub name and email.

**`sudo logout`** works like `shutdown`: in a minute with a warning, `now`, `+N`, at `HH:MM`, or `stop` to cancel.
Only desktop sessions end; SSH and text logins stay.

**Other desktops share your browsers and logins.** Firefox, Chromium-based browsers, OyNIx, chat apps, password
wallets and cloud CLIs now carry over between Halcyon and the extra desktops (ML4W, HyDE and the rest). HyDE installs
again after a failed first try.

**OyNIx 5.4.2** (from 5.2.4).

**Surface.** `[frontline-surface]` moves to the newest commit of Frontline's linux-surface fork. **Location**:
`sudo surface-contrib location --fix` sets up GeoClue (Wi-Fi positioning on every model, GPS on the LTE Surface Pro 5).
The **cameras** are steadier: surface-camd now creates its own loopback devices, so other apps' v4l2loopback settings
(OBS, Iriun) no longer take /dev/video50-52 away, and the old options file is removed on upgrade.

For the new apps: `sudo pacman -S font-manager frontline-rdp-express`.
Liquid Glass needs MacShell's extras: `sudo pacman -S awww qt6-shadertools inotify-tools zenity hyprsunset`.
HyperNeo v2 needs Summit's: `sudo pacman -S rofi cliphist hyprlock`.
