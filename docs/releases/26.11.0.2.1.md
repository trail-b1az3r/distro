## What's new in Rain Field 26.11.0.2.1

**Liquid Glass, with MacShell.** A new Halcyon theme that swaps the whole shell for
[MacShell](https://github.com/0-ss/MacShell) (MIT, bundled): a macOS Tahoe style menu bar, Dock, Control Center and
Spotlight in real-time Liquid Glass that refracts the wallpaper, with MacLock as the lock screen. Any other theme
brings Halcyon's shell back. Ctrl+Super+Shift+T opens the theme menu from either.

**Neumorph.** A light theme of soft clay: the dock, the bar, the clock and the cards are pressed out of one pale page
in its own colour, lit from the top left. It comes with its own icon pack and the rounded Open Runde font.

**Islands bar.** A fourth bar style in Settings > Bar: no strip across the screen, only floating pills (the window
title, the workspaces, the clock, the tray), each with a soft shadow.

**Fonts.** Font Manager is installed, and `halcyon fonts install FILE...` installs fonts, or a zip of a whole family,
for your user. Halcyon ships **RightFont**, "the most confusing font possible" from Right of the Bell (SIL OFL):
every letter only just looks like itself.

**nexora-msg 1.1.** Your iPhone's messages over Bluetooth or the cable, now with a remote desktop viewer for this PC
(over the new `nexora-rdp-express` server) and Tailscale, so both work away from home.

**Wi-Fi (nmx 1.1).** Captive-portal networks open their sign-in page instead of asking for a password, an optional
DNS-blocker bypass (off by default), and enterprise networks find their certificate on their own.

**Faster shut down, log out and wake-up.** Apps opened from the shell no longer hold the session open for systemd's
two-minute timeout.

**OyNIx 5.4.2** (from 5.2.4).

**Surface.** `[nexora-surface]` moves to the newest commit of Nexora's linux-surface fork: location setup (Wi-Fi
positioning, and GPS on LTE models) in `surface-contrib`, and surface-camd now creates its own loopback devices and
cleans up stale installs.

Updating from 26.11.0.2.0: `sudo pacman -Syu`. For the new apps: `sudo pacman -S font-manager nexora-rdp-express`.
Liquid Glass needs MacShell's extras: `sudo pacman -S awww qt6-shadertools inotify-tools zenity hyprsunset`.
