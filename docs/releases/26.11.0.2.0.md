## What's new in Rain Field

**Nexora Code, the built-in code editor.** VSCodium (the telemetry-free, MIT build of VS Code) made Nexora's own:
its own name, icon, settings and extensions folders (it never mixes with an installed VSCodium or VS Code), the Open
VSX marketplace, and Nexora's defaults (JetBrains Mono, smooth caret and scrolling, sticky scroll, fish in the
terminal, telemetry off). Its built-in **Nexora Material** theme takes Halcyon's colours, the theme's or the
wallpaper's, and follows them as they change; **Nexora Dark** and **Nexora Light** are there too. `nexora-code`, or
*Nexora Code* in the launcher.

**OyNIx is the default browser.** OyNIx, the Rust and QML browser with Material 3 Expressive, built-in content
blocking and no telemetry, opens web pages and links.

**Material 3 Expressive in every built-in app.** Welcome, the installer, the updater and the AI assistant: tonal
colour from the accent, the M3 shape scale, springy motion, a navigation rail, connected button groups and the
wavy loading indicator.

**Extra desktops never touch Halcyon.** ML4W, HyDE and SekaiPop install and run in a home of their own. Whatever
their installers write in your real home is moved into theirs and Halcyon's files are put back, they cannot replace
hyprland-plus with the stock compositor, their gsettings stay theirs, and login entries they add are set aside.
The login screen has a desktop switcher: scroll over the previews.

**Surface, from Nexora's own linux-surface fork.** The Surface kernel, iptsd, libwacom-surface and the fork's extras
come from the new `[nexora-surface]` repository, built from
[trail-b1az3r/linux-surface](https://github.com/trail-b1az3r/linux-surface). New: **cameras** on the Surface Pro
4/5/6 and Surface Book 1/2 (front, rear and IR, through surface-camd), a **speaker EQ** for the Pro 5 and 7+, and
modern **suspend** for the Pro 5 (`surface-contrib`).

**Nexora Write.** EPUB-MXL books (MDXL, EPUB and rich text in one file), headings that no longer show as links, and
reading positions and recent books kept by the Rust backend.

**hyprland-plus 0.57.2.p2** (Hyprland 0.57.2): the bulge between monitors now also shows when a tiled window is
dragged across.

**Halcyon.** The Sword Art Online theme's desktop widgets stand on the wallpaper like the game's HUD; Manga Ink's
workspace page turn is gone.

Updating from 26.11.0.1.x: `sudo pacman -Syu`, then `sudo pacman -S nexora-code oynix-bin` for the new apps.
On a Surface, `sudo nexora surface enable` moves it onto `[nexora-surface]`.
